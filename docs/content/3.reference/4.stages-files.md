A stages file names a crop's mature grids and says when each pixel appears.

```text [corn.stages]
# corn growth stages, derived from the mature plant
palette corn.palette
grid lower mature/lower.grid
grid upper mature/upper.grid
stages 8
shift 0 +2
shift 1 +2
shift 2 +1
shift 3 +1
map lower
.......55.......
...             (16 rows)
map upper
.....7.77.7.....
...             (16 rows)
```

Paths are relative to the stages file. Lines starting with `#` are comments.

## Directives

| directive | meaning |
|---|---|
| `palette FILE` | the family palette; required for `shift` |
| `grid lower FILE`, `grid upper FILE` | the mature grid of each half; a single-block crop has only `lower` |
| `stages N` | number of stages, 1 to 10; default 8 |
| `shift STAGE ±K` | at that stage, draw every pixel K shades lighter (+) or darker (−) within its material |
| `recolour STAGE FROM TO` | up to and including that stage, draw key FROM as key TO, before shifting |
| `map lower`, `map upper` | followed by 16 rows: the stage map of that half |

## Stage maps

A map is a 16×16 grid in which every opaque pixel of the mature grid carries the digit of the
first stage it appears in, and every transparent pixel is `.`. It must label exactly the mature
pixels. A pixel labelled 3 is drawn at stages 3 and later.

## Rules

- Stages are numbered from 0 to `stages` − 1.
- Every stage must have pixels in its lower half. An empty upper half is normal: the plant is one
  block tall at that stage.
- The upper half's bottom-row opaque columns must be a subset of the lower half's top row at
  every stage (the seam).
- A shift that collapses a ramp into one shade is reported; use `+1` on four-shade ramps.
- Shift and recolour errors are refused before anything is written.

## Output

`texturescript stages FILE.stages OUT_DIR` writes `OUT_DIR/stageN/lower.*` and `upper.*` (grid,
PNG, ×16 preview, ASCII) for every stage, and `OUT_DIR/strip.png`.
