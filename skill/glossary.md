## Glossary (what reads at 16px)

- **Stalk**: one 2px cane, unbroken, darkening downward.
- **Node**: where a leaf leaves the stalk; a 1-row darker band.
- **Leaf blade**: strap rising from a node, arching, drooping at the tip; a 2px stepped run up-and-out then down. Blades alternate sides.
- **Ear**: cob in a leaf axil, tilted slightly off the stalk; a short green-wrapped lump.
- **Husk**: leaf shades (b/c) covering the ear; kernels show only at the tip.
- **Silk**: 1 px of tassel shade at the ear tip, or omitted.
- **Tassel**: top spike plus thin branches; taller than wide, olive to straw.


Item-side terms:

- **Outline**: the one-pixel rim in the object's darkest ramp shade. Items have it; crop sprites never do.
- **Highlight**: 1 to 3 px of the lightest shade at the top-left of each form.
- **Kernel grain**: on an ear item, alternating adjacent ramp steps along the axis; never single dark dots.

Fruiting and vining crops (from the tomato test):

- **Vine**: the stalk of a climbing crop; 2 px wide on columns 7 and 8, may be its own olive material or leaf shades.
- **Fruit**: a 2 to 3 px blob in its own ramp, 3 to 4 shades, light shade top-left; reads as fruit only against green.
- **Cluster**: two or three fruit blobs touching or 1 px apart at one node; one cluster per 6 to 8 rows.
- **Calyx**: the 1 px of darkest leaf shade where a fruit meets its stem; the thing that makes a red blob a tomato.
- **Head**: a grain or seed head at the top of a stalk (wheat); a 2 to 3 px wide stepped run in the ripe ramp. Upright on wheat. A **drooping head** (rice, oats) hangs beside its stem: a 2 px wide, 3 to 4 px tall tilted blob, lightest at its top-left, one darker pixel at the hanging tip, on a 1 px arched stem.
- **Tiller**: one of several near-vertical 1 px stems a cereal throws from its base (wheat has five or six). A clump of tillers is the cereal grammar; it has no single 2 px stalk.
- **Culm**: a cereal stem that carries a head; the tiller that made it.
- **Panicle**: a branched, drooping head (rice). Several small heads on one arched stem; draw as a drooping head with one or two extra 1 px grain pixels along the arch.
- **Sheath**: where a grass blade wraps the stem before leaving it; at 16 px, the node of a cereal, no extra pixels.
- **Bulb**: the swollen base of an onion or garlic at the soil line; a 2 to 4 px wide dome in its own pale ramp, light shade top-left, one darker pixel where it meets the soil. Appears at stage 2 or 3, never in a sprout.
- **Tubular leaf**: an onion or chive leaf; a 1 px upright strand, 2 px through its lower half, in a fan of three to five from one base. A fan is neither a stalk nor a tiller clump; see root crops in the rules.
- **Root top**: the part of a carrot, beetroot or potato showing above the soil on rows 13 to 15; its own ramp, lightest at the centre of each plant.
